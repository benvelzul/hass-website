What is Dark Tourism?

Tourism has many forms, this trip will be focusing on dark tourism. Dark tourism is mainly in places that commemorate and remember disasters or tragedies. But the place where we are going is not a sad place; it marks the start of the age of discovery and what consequences arrived with it.

What is this place and what history is behind it?

The city of Seville is the place where Cristopher Columbus started his journey to later discover The Americas (now named North and South America). And where Ferdinand Magellan commenced his voyage of 5 ships to do the first circumnavigation and found a way to pass through The Americas and come back to Spain. Christopher Colombus was one of the greatest explorers of all time, discovering The Americas and conquering it. He lies in the Cathedral of Seville and the legend states that the restless ghost of Christopher Colombus is still roaming through the city. One of the most common sites in Seville is Muelle de las Mulas (Mules’ Wharf) which is the exact dock where Magellan never came back. Later Elcano (his vice captain) came back to Spain marking the first circumnavigation of the world.
